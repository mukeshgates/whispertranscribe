Word-Timestamp Transcriber

Drop in narration audio, get back a transcript with word-level timestamps — for syncing captions or effects to speech. Runs entirely in this browser tab; your audio is never uploaded anywhere.

🎙️ Drop audio here, or click to choose a file

First transcription downloads the model to this browser (60–250MB depending on quality) and caches it, so later runs on this device are instant to load. Everything — model download and transcription — happens locally in your browser tab via WebAssembly/WebGPU; nothing is sent to a server. Best in Chrome or Edge. Very long files (60+ min) may be slow on CPU-only machines — WebGPU (recent Chrome/Edge) is much faster if available.
